Before the Suit

THREE HONEST LINES, NO VIBES

My name is Lalith. I'm still a student — not a founder, not an engineer, not an expert. Not yet. Just someone who refuses to stop building.

I build because I need to know if an idea can exist. Not for productivity, not for followers — curiosity. Once a question enters my head, it won't leave until I've answered it in code.

School is one part of my life. When classes end, another education begins: docs, talks, bugs, and things nobody asked me to build.

This site isn't proof that I've become extraordinary. It's proof that I decided to start.
